About Us

Our mission is to provide a platform for Tamils to connect, learn, and grow together.

Whether you are a student, a professional, an entrepreneur, or a retiree, we have something to offer you. Our programs and initiatives are designed to serve the diverse needs of the Tamil community and support their social, cultural, and economic aspirations.

We are a non-profit organization dedicated to promoting the interests of Tamil people living in Canada and fostering a sense of unity and belonging within the community.

What We Do

Learn More What We Do And Get Involved

Community Development

At the Association of Tamil Community in Canada, we believe in community development. We work closely with our members to identify their needs and provide them with the resources and support they need to achieve their goals. Our programs and initiatives are designed to empower the community by providing them with access to knowledge, skills, and opportunities that help them grow and thrive.

LinkedIn Networking

We recognize the importance of networking in today's competitive job market. That's why we provide our members with the opportunity to connect with each other through our LinkedIn network. Whether you are a job seeker or an employer, our LinkedIn network can help you connect with the right people and expand your professional network.

Community Meet-ups

We organize community meet-ups, picnics, and club activities throughout the year to bring our members together and foster a sense of community spirit. These events provide our members with the opportunity to connect with each other, share their experiences, and build lasting relationships.

Student Clubs

We recognize the importance of supporting our young Tamil students in Canada. We have established student clubs in various universities and colleges across Canada to help our students connect with each other, share their experiences, and learn from each other.

Accommodation Help

We understand that finding suitable accommodation can be a challenge, especially for newcomers. That's why we provide our members with information and resources to help them find suitable accommodation that meets their needs.

Legal Help

We provide our members with access to legal resources and information to help them navigate the Canadian legal system. We work closely with legal professionals to provide our members with the information and support they need to protect their rights and interests.

Small Scale to Enterprise Business Shout-outs

We recognize the importance of entrepreneurship in today's economy. That's why we provide our members with a platform to showcase their businesses and services. Whether you are a small-scale entrepreneur or a large enterprise, we can help you promote your business to a wider audience.

Promotion for Art and Cultural Talents

We recognize the importance of promoting Tamil art and culture in Canada. That's why we organize events and initiatives to promote Tamil art, music, and culture in Canada. We believe that art and culture are powerful tools for building bridges between communities and promoting understanding and respect.

Helping Newcomers

We provide our newcomers with a warm welcome and help them settle in Canada. We provide them with information and resources to help them navigate the Canadian system and connect with the Tamil community in Canada.
